Shading: number of propagating modes (n² > 0 at some θ): white 0, light 1, darker 2. Bounding surfaces: cutoffs P=0, R=0, L=0; resonances R=∞ (ω=Ωe), L=∞ (ω=Ωi), S=0 (hybrids).
Root labels: R/L = limit at θ=0 (n²=R or L), O/X = limit at θ=90° (n²=P or RL/S). Red dash-dot lines: resonance cone tan²θres = −P/S. Dashed circle: vph = c. Types (Bellan Fig. 6.3): ellipsoid, dumbbell (θ < θres), wheel (θ > θres).
